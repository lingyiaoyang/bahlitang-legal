Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who this applies to

BAH LI TANG Manager is a private tool used by one person, the owner of the channel 8粒蛋 BAH LI TANG. It connects only to the owner's own accounts for that channel (TikTok @8lihtang, YouTube, a Facebook Page and its linked Instagram account). It does not collect data from any other person, and it has no other users.

What data the tool reads

  • Basic profile information of the owner's own account (name, ID, avatar).
  • The list of the owner's own public videos, with their publish time, title, duration and link.
  • Performance figures for those videos, such as views, likes, comments, shares and watch-time figures that the platform makes available to the account owner.

The tool does not read private messages, other people's accounts, followers' personal data or any data it was not given permission for.

What the data is used for

Only to produce the owner's own reports on how the channel's videos perform, so the owner can plan future videos. The data is not used for advertising, profiling or any automated decision about other people.

Where the data is stored

Access tokens and collected figures are stored on the owner's own computer. Tokens are kept in a file readable only by the owner and are never published. The data is not sold, rented, shared with or disclosed to any third party.

How long the data is kept

Figures are kept while the owner uses them for reporting. Where a platform's developer policy requires it, the tool re-checks authorisation and refreshes or removes data for videos that have been deleted.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time:

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services (or the equivalent screen in the app).
  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions. The tool's use of YouTube is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Facebook and Instagram: Settings → Business integrations, then remove the app.

After access is revoked, the stored figures and tokens for that platform are deleted from the owner's computer. To ask for deletion, email the address below.
